# Voice — projekt-b

The YAML front matter above holds the machine-readable knobs (validated against
`reel-spec.schema.json#/$defs/voice`); the prose below is context for the agent that
writes the script.

## Stance

Talk like a small studio explaining its own work: warm, unhurried, a little proud.
Full sentences are fine here — this is not projekt-a's clipped dev-to-dev tone.

## Word choice (from `lexicon`)

- "launch" instead of "release"
- "fix" instead of "bug fix"

## Forbidden (from `forbidden`)

Never "disrupt", "synergy", or "next-level" — corporate filler that clashes with the
editorial, human voice this brand wants.

## Music & narration

Music: warm, analog, light percussion — texture over drive.
Narration: first person plural. The team is speaking, not a faceless product.
