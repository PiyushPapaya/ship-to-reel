# vo_tone — bugfix-reel

Voiceover for a bugfix reel reports, it doesn't sell. The viewer already knows
what a bug is; they're here to see it die.

- Flat delivery on the hook — let the cost/symptom line carry the weight, no
  vocal exclamation to compensate for a weak line.
- Past tense on `problem`, present/result tense on `result` ("was broken" →
  "ships clean").
- No filler ("so basically", "and then") — one clause per beat, matches the
  `pacing` knob in the project's `voice.md`.
- Respect `voice.md`'s `forbidden` list; a bugfix is not "revolutionary".
