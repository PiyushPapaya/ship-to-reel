# music_mood — bugfix-reel

Default: tense-to-resolved arc, matching `tempo.energy_curve: build`.

- Under `hook`/`problem`: low, slightly dissonant or minimal-percussion bed —
  something's wrong.
- Under `code-diff`: a rise or filter-sweep — the fix landing.
- Under `result`/`outro`: the beat opens up, resolves cleanly. No triumphant
  horns — this is an engineering channel, not a movie trailer.

Overridden by the project's `voice.md` `music_mood` field when the two
disagree (brand+voice win tone, per the base-plate merge rule).
